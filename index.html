Locals and visitors loved the old Plaza store. When it closed, its manager, its employees and its best sellers moved into a third of the old space.

Frito Pie, served with a spoon

About 36,000 people a year eat one at the snack bar or on a bench in the Santa Fe Plaza.

Teresa Hernandez, a Woolworth's employee, invented this bag version in 1962.

  1. Slit open the side of a small bag of Fritos corn chips.
  2. Ladle in homemade chili.
  3. Top with grated cheddar and chopped onions.
  4. Serve with a spoon.

A Woolworth's closed. The best sellers stayed.

1935

The Woolworth's on the Santa Fe Plaza. Locals and tourists treasure it from 1935 on.

Aug 1997

Woolworth's announces it is closing all US stores, including the one on the Plaza.

May 1998

Deborah Potter, her husband Earl Potter and Mike Collins, a Woolworth's manager for 25 years, move the best selling items, the fixtures and the employees into a third of the old space. The first Five & Dime General Store opens there.

A battery or an aspirin, a short walk away

Each store keeps everyday needs in reach of locals and of people who work in busy tourist areas. A battery, an aspirin, an extension cord, toothpaste, a cold remedy.

Each store also provides jobs for over a dozen people.

Nine stores

The first is on the Santa Fe Plaza. The other eight are in busy visitor districts around the country.
